Part 4: Word Study

journey

Collocations: a long journey, a train journey, begin/start a journey, make a journey

Similar words: trip, travel

journey = the process of going from one place to another; trip = a visit or journey, often with a return; travel = travelling in general.

delay

Collocations: a short delay, a long delay, flight delay, train delay, because of a delay

Similar words: wait, hold-up

delay = something happens later than planned; wait = the action of staying until something happens; late = not on time.

comfortable

Collocations: a comfortable seat, comfortable clothes, feel comfortable, make someone comfortable

Similar words: relaxed, cosy

comfortable = pleasant to sit, wear, or be in; convenient = easy or useful; confident = sure about yourself.

Error Review and Explanation / 错题讲解

Part 3: Comprehension / 阅读理解

Q1 Student answer / 学生答案: She was going to the station.

Problem: The station was only the place where she took the train. Her real purpose was to visit her cousin.
问题：station 只是她坐火车的地点，不是她出行的真正目的。她真正要去看望 cousin。
Correct / 正确答案: She was going to visit her cousin.

Q4 Student answer / 学生答案: Yes, she missed lunch.

Problem: The text says she didn't miss lunch. The answer should be negative.
问题：原文说 she didn't miss lunch，说明她没有错过午餐，所以回答应该是否定。
Correct / 正确答案: No, she didn't. She arrived in time for lunch.

Q5 Student answer / 学生答案: comfortable

Problem: comfortable means pleasant to sit in, wear, or use. The word for many people in one place is crowded.
问题：comfortable 表示“舒服的”，比如座位舒服、衣服舒服。表示“人很多、拥挤的”应该用 crowded。

Part 6: Correct the Mistakes / 改错

  1. The seat is very comfortable, so I can sleep well.

    Confident describes a person who believes in themselves. Comfortable describes a seat, bed, clothes, or room.
    confident 描述“人很自信”；comfortable 描述座位、床、衣服、房间等“舒服”。

  2. We made a trip to London last summer.

    Make a trip is natural. Travel is usually a verb or an uncountable noun: I travelled to London. / I love travel.
    make a trip 是自然搭配。travel 常作动词或不可数名词：I travelled to London. / I love travel.

  3. There was a delay, so the train arrived at 9:00.

    A wait is something people do. A delay is the reason a train or flight is later than planned.
    wait 更偏“等待”这个动作；delay 表示火车、飞机等比计划时间晚了。

  4. My luggage is very heavy.

    Luggage is uncountable, so use is, not are.
    luggage 是不可数名词，所以谓语用 is，不用 are。

  5. The platform was crowded because there were too many people.

    Too many people makes a place crowded. Convenient means easy to use or close to what you need.
    人太多会让一个地方 crowded。convenient 表示“方便的”，比如地点近、使用起来容易。
