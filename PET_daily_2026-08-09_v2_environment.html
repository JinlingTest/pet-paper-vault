Part 2: Reading

Mia's class started a project about the environment. Their teacher asked them to protect their town by changing one daily habit. Mia decided to use less plastic and always carry a bottle from home. Her friend Leo tried to recycle paper and cans. The students also wanted to reduce food waste at lunch. A local shop helped them by putting up posters about pollution. After one month, the class saved water and energy, and they felt proud of their small changes.

Part 4: Word Study

protect

Collocations: protect the environment, protect animals, protect someone from danger

Similar words: save, guard

protect = keep something safe; save = stop something from being lost or wasted.

reduce

Collocations: reduce waste, reduce pollution, reduce the price, reduce the number of cars

Similar words: cut, lower, decrease

reduce = make something less; recycle = use something again in a new way.

waste

Collocations: food waste, plastic waste, waste time, waste money, reduce waste

Similar words: rubbish, trash

waste can be a noun or a verb: There is too much waste. / Don't waste water.

Error Review and Explanation / 错题讲解

Part 3: Comprehension / 阅读理解

Q1 Student answer / 学生答案: It was about the local shop.

Reading detail Problem: The local shop only helped with posters. The main project was about the environment.
问题：local shop 只是帮忙贴海报，不是项目主题。项目主题是 environment。
Correct / 正确答案: It was about the environment.

Q2 Student answer / 学生答案: She recycled paper and cans.

Who did it? Problem: Leo recycled paper and cans. Mia used less plastic and carried a bottle.
问题：这里混淆了人物。Leo 回收纸和罐子；Mia 少用塑料并自带水瓶。
Correct / 正确答案: She used less plastic and carried a bottle from home.

Q5 Student answer / 学生答案: protect

Meaning Problem: Protect means keep safe. Reduce means make something smaller or less.
问题：protect 是“保护”；reduce 才是“减少”。
Correct / 正确答案: reduce

Part 6: Correct the Mistakes / 改错

  1. We should protect the environment by using less plastic.

    Collocation Protect the environment is natural; recycle the environment is not.
    自然搭配是 protect the environment，不是 recycle the environment。

  2. There is too much pollution in the city.

    Grammar Pollution is uncountable, so use much.
    pollution 不可数，所以用 much，不用 many。

  3. Please recycle this bottle after you drink the water.

    Meaning A bottle can be recycled. Reduce means make less.
    瓶子可以被 recycle；reduce 是“减少”。

  4. It is a good habit to turn off the lights.

    Word choice Habit is personal. Custom is cultural or social.
    habit 是个人习惯；custom 是习俗。

  5. The local shop is near my school.

    Preposition Near is followed directly by a noun.
    near 后面直接接名词，不需要 of。
